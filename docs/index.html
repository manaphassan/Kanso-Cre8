Clinical Canvas

Structure & Surfaces

Creates layout grouping and hierarchy. Cards, nav topbars, content containers, and Prussian Blue footer surfaces.

Tokens: --ss-prussian-blue, --ss-blue
10% ± 5% Accent

Primary Focal Action

High-potency focal color strictly reserved for Primary CTAs, active navigation states, critical metrics, and focus indicators.

Tokens: --ss-azure, --ss-gold
CTA Competition Rule

Service category cards must never use solid accent backgrounds or accent-filled buttons. Only the dominant conversion action receives the uncompromised Primary Brand Accent treatment.
