The Kanso Zen Architecture

Engineered around simplicity, tactile responsiveness, and zero lock-in.

Pure Markdown-as-Database Storage Law

Zero SQL servers, zero SQLite binary files, zero Prisma ORM. 100% human-readable UTF-8 files with YAML frontmatter. Openable anytime in Obsidian, VS Code, or plain text editors. Works seamlessly in Dropbox, OneDrive, Google Drive, or Synology Drive.

Linear / Geist Studio System

Tailored studio tokens (var(--kanso-*)) featuring tactile dark/light modes, Neumorphic clay & obsidian extrusions, and razor-sharp 1px hairline borders.

Bullet Journal (BuJo) System

Daily rapid logging with standard BuJo symbols, automated morning task rollover reviews, 7-day completion cadence decks, and monthly creative retrospectives.

Notes Knowledge Engine

Integrated notes knowledge vault structured into 01_Fleeting/, 02_Literature/, and 03_Permanent/, paired with an instant clipboard scratchpad.

Chronometer & Focus Radio

Tactile billable timer with auto earnings accrual, 15-minute smart idle detection, and retro cassette focus radio with mechanical 33 RPM spool animation.
