Privacy Policy for Kanso Cre8 (簡素)

Effective Date: September 1, 2026

Kanso Cre8 is an offline-first, local-first creative operations vault, client hub, and Zettelkasten knowledge engine designed for freelance creators and independent studios.

1. The Kanso Local-First Data Law

We believe your creative business data belongs entirely to you:

  • 100% Plain Filesystem: All projects, client profiles, invoices, and atomic notes are stored locally on your machine in open UTF-8 Markdown (.md) files with YAML frontmatter.
  • Zero Binary Database Servers: Absolutely no SQL Server, SQLite binary files, Prisma ORM, or cloud databases are used.
  • Zero Telemetry & Trackers: Kanso Cre8 does not collect, track, profile, or transmit your activity, files, or client data to third-party ad networks or data brokers.

2. Cloud Sync & Multi-Platform Independence

Because your vault lives entirely as standard directories and files, synchronization is managed under your own control through your choice of file sync providers (Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive, Synology Drive, or private Git repositories). No intermediary cloud server has access to your vault content.

3. AI Integrations

When you optionally enable AI assistance (such as Google Gemini for copy generation or briefing assistance), requests are sent securely via direct client-side or local API calls using your personal API key. We do not store or retain your prompts on any intermediary server.

4. Third-Party Focus Streams

The Retro Cassette Focus Radio connects to publicly accessible ambient audio streams (Chillhop Cafe, Nightwave Plaza, SomaFM Groove Salad, Parisian Jazz, Zen Alpha Focus). Network requests are strictly limited to audio stream playback via the standard Web Audio API.

5. Data Retention & Full Ownership

You can inspect, backup, move, or delete your entire vault at any time simply by opening your file manager. Your data remains 100% human-readable and Obsidian / VS Code compatible forever.
